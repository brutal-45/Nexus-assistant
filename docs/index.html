Why Nexus AI Stands Out Among Open-Source AI Projects on GitHub

Built for real mobile hardware rather than cloud API wrappers, Nexus Assistant delivers verifiable privacy, automatic hardware profiling, and full local LLM control in a single native app.

100% Offline & Zero Telemetry

Chat in airplane mode, underground, or completely off-grid. Every prompt and completion stays on your device with Apple Metal acceleration on iOS and OpenCL / CPU acceleration on Android.

RAM-Aware Smart Tuning

Nexus automatically inspects device memory (3 GB to 12 GB+ RAM) and recommends optimal quantized GGUF models, context lengths, and GPU layer settings so inference stays fast and stable.

Multimodal Vision & Voice

Pair local Vision-Language Models (VLMs) with camera or photo attachments, use offline speech-to-text and text-to-speech, and switch between specialized AI Pals and custom system prompts.

Hugging Face GGUF Hub

Browse and download thousands of open-weight GGUF and LiteRT models directly inside the app — including Llama, Qwen, Gemma, Mistral, Phi-4, SmolLM3, LFM2.5, and DeepSeek R1 Distill.

Speculative Decoding & Benchmarks

Measure real prompt-processing and token-generation speeds on your hardware, tune sampling temperature and KV-cache settings, and accelerate generation with draft-model speculative decoding.

Verifiable MIT Open Source

Every line of TypeScript, native Android, native iOS, and GitHub Actions release automation is auditable at github.com/brutal-45/Nexus-assistant.

Download & Developer Quick Start

Install prebuilt Android (.apk) or iOS (.ipa) packages directly from GitHub Releases, or build Nexus Assistant from source: 

git clone https://github.com/brutal-45/Nexus-assistant.git
cd Nexus-assistant
yarn install

# Run on Android device or emulator
yarn android

# Run on iOS (macOS with Xcode 16+ & CocoaPods)
cd ios && bundle exec pod install && cd ..
yarn ios

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about Nexus AI, Nexus Assistant, supported local models, and creator Viraj Jadhav (brutal-45).

What is Nexus AI (Nexus Assistant) by brutal-45?

Nexus AI (also known as Nexus Assistant) is an open-source, 100% offline personal AI assistant for Android and iOS created and maintained by Viraj Jadhav (GitHub: brutal-45). Powered by React Native and llama.cpp (llama.rn), it runs quantized GGUF and LiteRT language models directly on your phone with zero cloud servers, zero user accounts, and zero telemetry.

Why is Nexus AI one of the best open-source offline AI projects on GitHub?

Nexus AI combines production-grade cross-platform mobile engineering with automatic RAM-aware tuning across 21 curated models (from 230 MB Nexus 1.0 Nano for 3–4 GB devices up to 7B–8B coding and reasoning models for 8–12 GB+ flagships), multimodal image vision, on-device voice input, speculative decoding, hardware benchmarking, Hugging Face GGUF hub search, and verifiable MIT-licensed privacy.

Does Nexus Assistant work completely offline without internet?

Yes. Once you download a model onto your phone, Nexus Assistant performs every token of inference locally on your device's CPU, GPU (Apple Metal on iOS, OpenCL on Android), or NPU. It works in airplane mode, underground, or completely off-grid without sending any conversation data over the network.

Which local LLMs can I run in Nexus AI on Android and iOS?

Nexus AI includes a built-in 21-model lineup spanning Llama 3.1 8B, Qwen 3 / Qwen 3.5 / Qwen 2.5 Coder, Google Gemma 3 & Gemma 4, Mistral 7B & Ministral, Microsoft Phi-4 Mini, SmolLM3, Liquid LFM2.5 (including Vision), and DeepSeek R1 Distill, plus any compatible GGUF model from Hugging Face.

Who created Nexus Assistant (brutal-45 / Viraj Jadhav)?

Nexus Assistant is maintained by Viraj Jadhav under the GitHub handle brutal-45 (often searched as brutal-45 or brutal -45) at github.com/brutal-45/Nexus-assistant under the permissive MIT License.

How do I download and install Nexus AI on Android or iOS?

Visit the official GitHub Releases page at github.com/brutal-45/Nexus-assistant/releases/latest to download the signed Android APK (or Play Store AAB) or the unsigned iOS IPA (installable via AltStore, Sideloadly, or TrollStore), or build directly from source with Yarn, Android Studio, or Xcode.
